Under the Hood Uploader

Under the Hood Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner to upload and schedule videos to the Under the Hood YouTube channel. It uses the YouTube API Services. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account or data.

By using this tool, the channel owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service.

The tool also sends finished videos as drafts to the owner's TikTok inbox through the TikTok Content Posting API; the owner reviews and publishes them in the TikTok app.
